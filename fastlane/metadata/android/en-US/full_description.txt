Calculator is a clean, fast and fully offline calculator with a scientific mode, a unit converter, everyday tools and a date calculator in one small app. No ads, no tracking, no accounts. Free and open source.

CALCULATOR
• Live result preview while you type
• Scientific mode: sin, cos, tan, ln, log, √, x², xʸ, 1/x, π and e, in degrees or radians
• Rotate your phone to open scientific mode automatically
• History saved on your device; tap an entry to reuse it
• Long-press the display to copy, paste or share a result
• Swipe the display to delete the last digit

UNIT CONVERTER
11 categories with live results for every unit at once: length, weight, temperature, area, volume, speed, time, data, fuel economy, pressure and energy.

TOOLS
• Discount & Tax: sale price, savings and VAT / GST / sales tax
• Tip & Split: tip amount and how much each person pays
• Percentage: X% of Y, X as a percent of Y, and percent change
• Loan / EMI: monthly payment, total interest and total paid
• BMI: metric or imperial, with the healthy range for your height

DATE CALCULATOR
• Days between two dates, in years, months, weeks and days
• Exact age from a date of birth and days until your next birthday

MAKE IT YOURS
• Light, dark or system theme with 6 accent colors
• Number formats for every region: 1,234.56 · 1.234,56 · 1 234,56 · 12,34,567.89
• Choose the number of decimal places
• Turn key vibration on or off

PRIVACY
Calculator has no internet permission. It does not collect, store or share any personal data. Your history and settings stay on your device.
